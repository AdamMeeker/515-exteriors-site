The Quick Wins

What we can build. How fast. What it changes.

We start here — fast turnarounds, real results, zero disruption to how you work.

Subcontractor COI Tracker

SharePoint list + Power Automate. Sends 30-day and 7-day renewal reminders automatically. You get an internal alert if a sub shows up with an expired cert.

~1 day → No more manual expiration tracking

Iowa Sales Tax Flag

Any AccuLynx job tagged "Repair" gets automatically checked for a sales tax line item. Missing it? You get flagged before it goes out.

~2 days → Kill the Excel spreadsheet. Zero compliance risk.

Material Return Credit Tracker

Log a return, get a reminder if the credit hasn't shown up from the supplier within a set window. No more chasing credits by memory.

~4 hours → No more lost credits

Post-Job Thank You Automation

When a final invoice is marked paid in AccuLynx, a personalized thank-you email goes out automatically with your Google review link and social media. Set once, runs forever.

~4 hours → 100% consistent follow-through, zero effort

Change Order Documentation

Structured form (web or M365) that captures material cost, crew pay, customer sign-off, and schedule impact in one place — attached to the AccuLynx job automatically.

~1–2 days → Full paper trail on every change

Lead Qualification Form

Smart intake form on your website. Collects property address, insurance status, timeline, and budget before Chris or Georgia touches it. High-value leads pushed directly into AccuLynx.

~3–4 days → Pre-screen leads before anyone lifts a finger

Homeowner Schedule Notification

When a job gets scheduled in AccuLynx, a notification goes out to the homeowner automatically — crew info, what to expect, date and time.

~1 day → Professional touchpoint, zero manual effort

Bank Deposit Workflow

Dedicated shared mailbox for deposit photos. Image lands → auto-tagged in OneDrive → QB entry simplified. Kills the "email to self" step.

~1 day → Four steps becomes one

Double-Entry Elimination

When a bill or receipt is entered in QuickBooks, Power Automate automatically copies it to the correct AccuLynx job folder. Georgia touches it once.

~2–3 days → Touch it once. Done.

Commission Calculator

Pulls job cost and revenue from AccuLynx, calculates profitability and crew commission automatically. No spreadsheet math.

~1–2 days → Accurate commissions, no manual math

Invoice Pre-Check

Before an invoice goes out, an automated checklist runs: deposit applied? Photos attached? Change orders included? Tax on repair jobs? Flags anything missing.

~1 day → Nothing slips through
